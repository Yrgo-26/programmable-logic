\chapter{Variables and the hardware underneath}
\label{c5:ch}

{\large\itshape The last construct left, and what the toolchain makes of all of it.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \code{variable}: process-local storage, updating immediately where a \code{signal}
    schedules.
  \item Why the same accumulation gives two different answers depending on which one you reach for.
  \item What an FPGA physically consists of: lookup tables and flip-flops, and nothing that is a
    gate.
  \item The critical path and Fmax, and what puts a speed limit on a clock.
  \item The latch you describe by accident, and why it is only a warning.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

This chapter's point is in its first worked example: code that looks correct, simulates without
complaint, and is wrong. The only way to see why is to \emph{trace} it rather than read it, one
loop iteration at a time, all the way to the wrong answer. Do that on paper before you read the
correction.
