\chapter{The CAN controller II: reception and arbitration}
\label{c17:ch}

{\large\itshape Completing \code{can_controller}. After this chapter the CAN half of the project is
finished.\par}

\begin{chapterbox}{In this chapter}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The receive role: the timing of \code{rx_shift_reg}'s enable, and the single cycle of
    pre-emption.
  \item Reassembling a received frame, and feeding \code{crc15} on the receive side.
  \item A clean slate between frames, and why both shift registers are held in reset while the bus is
    idle.
  \item Detecting lost arbitration, checked bit by bit across the arbitration field.
  \item Running \code{can_controller_tb} all the way through, transmission and reception together.
\end{itemize}
\end{chapterbox}

\Chapref{c16:ch} built the transmit path. This chapter completes \code{can_controller} with the
\code{role = '0'} path (reception) and the arbitration logic deciding which of two competing
transmitters gets to carry on. The frame layout, the state sequence and the per-field group sizes are
the same as in \chapref{c16:ch}; the receive side merely reads every field back instead of driving
it.
